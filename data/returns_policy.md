# UrbanThread Return and Refund Policy

## Return window
You can return items within 30 days of the delivery date.

## Condition of items
Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have the original tags attached.

## Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, underwear, socks, and items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.

## How to start a return
Go to urbanthread.example/returns and enter your order number and email. For US orders we email you a prepaid return label. International customers pay for their own return shipping.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive your return.

## Exchanges
Size exchanges are free within 30 days, subject to stock availability.

## Damaged or wrong items
If you received a damaged or incorrect item, email support@urbanthread.example within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement at no cost.
