# UrbanThread Product FAQ

## Sizing
Every product page has a size chart. Our clothing fits true to size. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up.

## Materials
- T-shirts: 100% organic cotton.
- Hoodies: 80% cotton and 20% recycled polyester.
- Denim jackets: 98% cotton and 2% elastane.

## Care instructions
Machine wash cold, tumble dry low, and do not bleach. Turn hoodies inside out before washing.

## Sold-out items
Click the "Notify Me" button on the product page and we will email you when the item is back in stock.

## Gift cards
We sell digital gift cards in $25, $50, and $100 amounts. They are delivered by email instantly and never expire.

## Physical stores
UrbanThread is an online-only store. We do not have physical shops.

## Account and password
Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page to reset your password. Guest checkout is also available.

## Loyalty program
Earn 1 point for every $1 you spend. Every 100 points can be redeemed for a $5 reward.
