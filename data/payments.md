# UrbanThread Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Shop Pay. We do not offer cash on delivery.

## When you are charged
Your payment method is charged when you place your order.

## Discount codes
Only one discount code can be used per order. Discount codes cannot be combined with other discount codes.

## Sales tax
Sales tax for US orders is calculated at checkout.
