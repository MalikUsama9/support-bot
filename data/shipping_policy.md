# UrbanThread Shipping Policy

## Processing time
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders placed after 2 PM EST on Friday ship the following Monday.

## Domestic shipping (USA)
- Standard shipping: 5-7 business days, $4.99. Standard shipping is free on orders over $50.
- Express shipping: 2-3 business days, $12.99.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia. International shipping takes 10-15 business days and costs a flat $19.99. Customers are responsible for any customs duties and import taxes.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed to you within 24 hours after your order ships.

## Lost or delayed packages
If your US order has not arrived 10 business days after it shipped, contact support at support@urbanthread.example with your order number.
