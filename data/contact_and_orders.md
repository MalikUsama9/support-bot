# UrbanThread Contact and Order Help

## Support hours
Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST. We are closed on weekends and US public holidays.

## How to contact us
Email support@urbanthread.example or use the live chat on our website during support hours. We reply to emails within 24 business hours.

## Changing or cancelling an order
Orders can be changed or cancelled within 2 hours of being placed. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed.

## Checking your order status
Use the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email, or visit urbanthread.example/track and enter your order number.

## Email response time
We reply to emails within 24 business hours. Email support@urbanthread.example and our team will get back to you within 24 business hours.
